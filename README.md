# AI Automation Hub

Create a modern SaaS landing page for an AI Automation Agency.

The page should promote a free consultation for businesses wanting to automate operations using AI.

Style:

- Modern

- Premium startup aesthetic

- Similar to Linear, Stripe, or Vercel

Sections:

Hero

Headline:

Automate Your Business With AI

Subheadline:

We build AI workflows that save hours every week by connecting your favorite apps.

CTA button:

Book Free Consultation

Lead Form

Fields:

- Full Name

- Business Email

- Company Name

- Website

- Company Size (dropdown)

- Industry

- Describe Your Biggest Business Challenge (textarea)

Submit button:

Get My AI Audit

Responsive design

Use React and Tailwind.
